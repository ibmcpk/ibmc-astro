Unlike textbook charts, the table given in the myp eassessment omits color-coding for metals, metalloids, and nonmetals, trend arrows, electron configurations, ionization energies, and lists of common polyatomic ions or charges.

Why?

Having fewer labels on the page does not mean the test is easier. It actually means students need to **remember more** before they start. When the labels are gone, students cannot rely on them as a crutch to find the answers. They must bring that information with them into the exam.
